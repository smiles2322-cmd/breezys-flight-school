    How to Play

    • Flap: tap the big FLAP button, or press Space. Flapping lifts Breezy up.
    • Steer: drag the thumb stick, tilt the phone (turn it on in Settings), or use the arrow keys or W A S D.
    • Climb and dive: push up to climb and down to dive. Climbing slows you down, diving speeds you up.
    • Glide: let go and Breezy glides gently. If she goes too slow she just floats down softly.
    • No crashing: touching water makes a happy splash and bounce. Touching grass is a landing. Flap to take off again.
    • Lost? Follow the arrow, or tap the speaker button (or press H) and Grandma tells you where to go.
    • Blind & Low Vision mode: each goal makes a beeping sound. It moves left and right with the goal, gets higher when the goal is above you, and beeps faster as you get close. A whoosh means you are low, so flap!
    • Pause: the pause button, or Escape.
